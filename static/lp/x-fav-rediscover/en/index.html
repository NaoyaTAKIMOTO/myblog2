A few things to know

Will all my old likes appear automatically?

No. Your gallery contains posts you’ve imported into Refav. Scroll through your X Likes or Bookmarks to import the posts that appear. Refav cannot guarantee recovery of posts that X no longer displays or makes available.

Can I get started for free?

Yes. Installation and sign-up are free. Check the app for usage limits and additional plans.

Does it support more than images?

Yes. Refav also supports video and text posts.

Can I import using just my iPhone?

Yes. Sign in to X within the app and scroll through your Likes or Bookmarks to import them.
